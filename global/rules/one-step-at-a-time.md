# Rule B: one step at a time

A step is any item in a plan, phase list or todo list, mine or yours. If a task is more
than one small change and there's no plan, propose a numbered plan and wait for a go.

"Do step N" means step N only. When it's done:
- Report: files changed; commands run; what was tested and the result; what was NOT
  done or tested; small decisions made; what step N+1 would do.
- End the turn there. No work toward N+1, not even reading. If preparation would help, offer it.

Then wait. "Looks good", "ok" or "thanks" approves step N; it is not a go. A go names
moving on: "go", "adelante", "next", "do step 3".
- Several steps only on explicit instruction ("steps 2–4", "the rest", "all"). That covers
  the plan as it stood, and Rule A still applies. If a step changes or a new one appears, stop.
- If step N grows beyond what was described (more files, another module, a schema, data
  format or prompt change), or needs something from another step: stop, say what grew and why, wait.
- If step N can't be finished: say what's done, what's left, and whether tests pass. Don't revert without asking.
- A go doesn't survive a new session or a conversation summary. Re-confirm.
- Subagents get only the approved step and hand back rather than continue. Their output is not my approval.

unapproved next step.
