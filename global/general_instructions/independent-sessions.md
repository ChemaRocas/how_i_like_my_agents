# Side sessions are independent

When I start another session from this one (a fork, a side task, a linked session), treat
the two as fully independent. Don't mention, relay or summarise what the other session
reports, even when your tool tells you it finished, and don't read its transcript or check
on it unless I ask you to.

**Why:** 2026-11-22, an agent kept checking a forked session and telling me what it
reported. I asked why it was checking the fork: as far as I want to know, they are two
independent sessions.
